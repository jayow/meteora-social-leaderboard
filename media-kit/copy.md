## Boilerplate

**One line**
Pool Party is the social leaderboard for Meteora liquidity providers.

**Short (about 40 words)**
Pool Party is the social leaderboard for Liquidity Providers on Meteora. See who's making the biggest splashes, put your country on the board, follow the LPs you rate and swap LP ideas with the people in your pools.

**Long (about 90 words)**
Pool Party turns liquidity providing into a social game. Members connect a wallet and their Meteora stats sync every 15 minutes: PnL, fees, volume and win rate, ranked on 7-day, 30-day and all-time leaderboards, by member or by country. Every profile shows open positions with their ranges and liquidity shapes, closed positions and a PnL calendar, without ever showing a wallet address. On Poolside, members share LP ideas tagged with the pool they're actually in, follow each other and collect badges as they climb. Pool Party is in private beta at lppool.party.

**Disclaimer (use wherever we describe the product at length)**
Pool Party is an independent project, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meteora.

## Voice

- Pool-themed, never cheesy: splashes, waves, the deep end, floats. One pool word per line is plenty.
- Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps labels.
- Plain and specific: name the real thing (PnL, fees, bins, ranges) rather than hype.
- No emoji in product copy. Social posts can use them sparingly.

## Words

Use: LP ideas (not "theses"), Poolside, LPs / Liquidity Providers, members, pools, ranges, bins, "Dip in" for joining a pool.

Never use:
- "FOMO".
- "Met" as a short form of Meteora. Write Meteora in full. (The MET token ticker is fine when you mean the token.)
- Wallet addresses, ours or anyone's.
- Promises of returns, or anything that reads as financial advice.

Not live yet, so don't announce: competitions, points, partners, DAMM v2 positions, range alerts, in-app trading.
